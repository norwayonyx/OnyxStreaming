# OnyxStreaming

Turn your PC audio into an internet radio stream your friends can listen to — great for sharing music in 3DXChat and similar.

## Download

Grab the latest version from the [**Releases**](../../releases/latest) page:

- **OnyxStreaming-Native-Setup-x.y.z.exe** — installer (recommended)
- **OnyxStreaming Native-x.y.z-win.zip** — portable (unzip and run, no install)

## How it works

1. Open OnyxStreaming.
2. Press **Go live**. The app captures your PC audio and starts a stream.
3. Share the public link it gives you — anyone can open it in a browser or media player to listen.

You can stream your **whole system** audio or just a **single app**, and adjust the output **volume** in the app.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

## "Unknown publisher" warning

OnyxStreaming isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may show a warning the first time you run it. Click **More info → Run anyway**. This is normal for small indie apps.

## Verify your download (optional)

Each release lists SHA-256 checksums. To check a file in PowerShell:
`Get-FileHash .\OnyxStreaming-Native-Setup-x.y.z.exe -Algorithm SHA256`

If the output matches the hash on the release page, your download is intact.
